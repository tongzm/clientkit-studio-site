Reseller Inventory & Profit Tracker

Price inventory before you buy and track actual profit after fees, shipping and overhead.

Your sale price is not your business profit. The Reseller Inventory & Profit Tracker helps part-time resellers calculate prices before buying inventory and track what the business actually keeps after item costs, marketplace fees, shipping, sale costs and overhead.

Price: US$7 one-time. No subscription and no live marketplace connection.

Who it is for

For part-time eBay, Poshmark, Mercari and other marketplace sellers who use notebooks or generic spreadsheets and want more control over pricing and profit records. It is not designed for sellers who need automatic cross-listing, sales imports or marketplace synchronization.

Calculate the price before you buy

Use the pricing calculator to test break-even and target-margin prices using your own item-cost, fee, shipping and overhead assumptions. This helps you evaluate a potential purchase before committing cash to inventory. Fee percentages are editable examples, not claims about your account's current fees.

Track profit after the sale

The workbook connects inventory and sales records to calculate total item cost, platform fees, net revenue, net profit, margin, ROI and days to sell. The Expenses sheet records packaging, software, storage and other overhead so the dashboard can show profit after business expenses as well as item-level results.

What is included

• Example XLSX workbook populated entirely with fictional data.

• Blank XLSX workbook with prepared formulas, validation and layout.

• 500 inventory rows, 500 sales rows and 300 expense rows.

• Pricing Calculator and Dashboard sheets.

• Five-page searchable Quick Start PDF and two-page printable Weekly Reseller Checklist PDF.

• README and internal-use license for one business.

Compatibility and limitations

The files and formulas were checked programmatically. Live Google Sheets and LibreOffice behavior has not been verified. Use desktop spreadsheet software that supports XLSX formulas, charts and data validation. No automatic import, bank sync, live marketplace connection or cross-listing is included.

This organizer is not tax, legal, accounting or financial advice. Review the calculations and recordkeeping requirements for your own circumstances.
