What the calculator includes

Net profit = item sale price + buyer-paid shipping − percentage fees − fixed fees − item cost − seller shipping and packaging − other costs. Enter your combined marketplace and payment-processing percentage in one field, and all fixed transaction fees in the fixed-fee field. Select the checkbox only when your percentage fees also apply to buyer-paid shipping.

Maximum item cost for target profit = item sale price + buyer-paid shipping − estimated fees − seller shipping and packaging − other costs − your target net profit. A negative result means the target is not reachable under the entered assumptions, even if the item were free.

The break-even item price is the estimated price at which net profit is zero; reverse-calculated item prices are floored at US$0. The target-margin price solves for the net margin on total item-plus-shipping revenue. Results are estimates, not tax or accounting advice; include any applicable taxes or extra costs in the appropriate fields.
