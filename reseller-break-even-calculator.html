What the calculator includes

Net profit = sale price − percentage fees − fixed fees − item cost − shipping and packaging − other costs. Enter your combined marketplace and payment-processing percentage in one field, and all fixed transaction fees in the fixed-fee field.

The break-even price is the estimated sale price at which net profit is zero. The target-margin price solves for the net margin you enter. Results are estimates, not tax or accounting advice; include any applicable taxes or extra costs in the appropriate fields.
